Henrisol Properties Ltd is the real estate arm of OA Group Nigeria — providing professional property services to individuals, corporates, and diaspora investors across Nigeria.

Who We Are

The real estate arm of OA Group Nigeria

Henrisol Properties Ltd was established as the real estate and property services company within OA Group Nigeria — providing the group's ecosystem with a professional property capability that complements accounting, consulting, financial services, and contracting across the portfolio.

Within the OA Group ecosystem, Henrisol plays a specific property role: providing real estate investment vehicles for MOL investment clients, development and property management services for commercial clients, and diaspora property investment structures that leverage the group's financial services and advisory capabilities.

The purple palette is deliberate — in a Nigerian real estate sector dominated by conventional blues and greens, Henrisol's distinct visual identity signals premium positioning. Aspiration, distinction, and quality — in the properties we represent and the service we deliver.
