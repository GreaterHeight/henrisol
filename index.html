For Diaspora Investors

Invest in Nigerian property — from the UK, US, or Canada

Henrisol provides a structured, remote-accessible real estate investment service specifically designed for Nigerian diaspora investors. We manage every step — from property identification and legal verification through purchase, management, and income reporting — so you can invest with confidence from abroad.

01
Portfolio Consultation

A dedicated advisor understands your investment objectives, budget, timeline, and risk profile — and recommends the appropriate Henrisol investment package.

02
Property Selection & Legal Verification

We present verified investment opportunities with full legal documentation — Certificate of Occupancy (C of O), survey plans, and title verification — before any commitment is made.

03
Secure Remote Purchase

Transaction management with verified payment channels, Power of Attorney where required, and full documentation provided digitally and by courier.

04
Ongoing Management & Reporting

Professional property management — tenant sourcing, maintenance, and rent collection — with quarterly digital financial reports and on-demand updates.
