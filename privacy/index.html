Henrisol Properties Ltd ("Henrisol," "we," "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who interact with our website and real estate services. This policy complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

1. Who We Are

Henrisol Properties Ltd is a real estate company registered in Nigeria (RC: [CAC: RC-XXXXXX]), with its registered office at 4, Aderoju Adewuyi Street, Off Alfred Olaiya Street, Opebi-Ikeja, Lagos State, Nigeria. We are a member of OA Group Nigeria.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact and identity information submitted through enquiry forms and property viewings
  • Property search and preference data
  • Investment enquiry details and financial preferences
  • Browser, device, and usage data collected automatically (with consent for analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to property and investment enquiries
  • To coordinate property viewings and sales transactions
  • To send property alerts where you have registered interest
  • To deliver property management and facilities management services under formal agreements
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Delivery of property services under formal agreements
  • Legitimate interests: Responding to enquiries, property matching, business development
  • Consent: Marketing communications and property alerts
  • Legal obligation: Anti-money laundering compliance on property transactions

5. Property Transaction Compliance

Nigerian real estate transactions are subject to anti-money laundering requirements. We are required to verify the identity of buyers, sellers, and investors in accordance with applicable regulations. Identity documents collected for this purpose are retained for the minimum period required by law.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with legal professionals and third parties involved in property transactions (with your knowledge), regulatory bodies as required, and other OA Group members where you specifically request group services.

7. Your Rights

Under NDPR/NDPA you have rights to access, correct, erase, and object to processing of your data. Contact: info@henrisol.oagroupng.com
